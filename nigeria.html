Important Aviation Organizations

Different organizations perform different responsibilities within Nigeria's aviation system.

🏛️

Nigeria Civil Aviation Authority

NCAA

The NCAA is Nigeria's civil aviation regulatory authority. Its responsibilities include aviation safety oversight and personnel licensing among other regulatory functions.

🏢

Federal Airports Authority of Nigeria

FAAN

FAAN manages airport facilities and supports safe, secure and efficient airport operations across its airport network.

📡

Nigerian Airspace Management Agency

NAMA

Airspace management is a major part of aviation. Air navigation services help aircraft operate within an organized national airspace system.

🌦️

Nigerian Meteorological Agency

NiMet

Aviation weather information is important because pilots and aviation organizations need weather information when planning and managing flights.

🔎

Nigerian Safety Investigation Bureau

NSIB

Aviation safety investigation organizations study accidents and serious incidents to help improve transportation safety.

🎓

Nigerian College of Aviation Technology

NCAT

Aviation education and professional training are important parts of developing the people who work throughout the aviation sector.

Things Every Future Pilot Should Know

A pilot's knowledge goes far beyond simply knowing how an aircraft flies.

🧠 Aviation Knowledge

Pilots study subjects including aircraft systems, aerodynamics, weather, navigation, human factors, regulations and operational decision-making.

🌦️ Weather

Weather can affect visibility, aircraft performance, flight planning and operational decisions, making meteorological knowledge important.

📡 Communication

Aviation involves communication between pilots, air traffic services and other aviation professionals using standardized procedures and terminology.

🛡️ Safety

Safety is not just one subject. It is built into training, decision-making, procedures, aircraft maintenance and the wider aviation system.

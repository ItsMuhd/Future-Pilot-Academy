COCKPIT KNOWLEDGE

Know Your Instruments

The basic flight instruments provide information about important flight parameters such as airspeed, altitude, attitude, heading and vertical movement.

💨

Airspeed

The airspeed indicator shows indicated airspeed. It is one of the core instruments found in traditional flight-instrument arrangements.

🌅

Attitude

The attitude indicator gives a direct visual reference of aircraft pitch and bank relative to the horizon.

📏

Altitude

The altimeter provides an indication of altitude based on atmospheric pressure information.

🧭

Heading

The heading indicator provides a directional reference, helping represent the aircraft's orientation.

📈

Vertical Speed

The vertical speed indicator provides an indication of the rate at which altitude is changing.

🔄

Turn Coordinator

The turn coordinator provides information related to turning motion and coordination.

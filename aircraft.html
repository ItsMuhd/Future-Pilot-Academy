Flight Science

The Four Forces of Flight

Aircraft flight involves four major forces. Understanding them is an important part of basic aviation science.

⬆️

Lift

The aerodynamic force that acts generally upward and supports an aircraft in flight.

⬇️

Weight

The force caused by gravity acting on the aircraft and its contents.

➡️

Thrust

The forward force produced by an aircraft's propulsion system.

⬅️

Drag

Aerodynamic resistance that acts against an aircraft's motion through the air.
